# Saved sessions

A **saved session** is a deliberate snapshot of every tab in a browser window,
kept as one item. Use it to clear a pile of tabs without losing them.

## Saving a session

From the extension popup you get two buttons, and the difference between them is
what happens to the window:

- **Save session & keep open (N tabs)** — snapshot it and carry on working.
- **Save session & close (N tabs)** — snapshot it and clear the window.

Every open tab is captured, including which window each tab belonged to, so the
layout can be rebuilt on restore. A session snapshots every tab — including
browser-internal pages — but only real `http(s)` links are ever made clickable.

:::note
On the **& close** path, the window closes only **after** the server confirms
the save. A failed save can never cost you your tabs. If a save is taking a
while you'll see `Saving N tabs… your tabs stay open until the save is
confirmed.` instead of a bare spinner.
:::

Session saves are also much faster than they used to be — the extension now
reuses the credential it already holds instead of negotiating a new one on every
click. On the developer's machine a warm save measured around 50 ms, down from
roughly 420 ms; your numbers will differ, but the gesture should feel immediate.

## Restoring a session

In the app's **Saved sessions** view, each saved session can be restored as:

- **A window** — reopen all its tabs together, rebuilding the original grouping, or
- **A tab group / individual tabs** — open tabs one at a time instead of the whole set.

away without opening the sessions view at all.

## Naming and tidying up

- **Rename** a session inline at any time — a snapshot called "Window 3" ages
  badly.
- Or let the **AI name it** from what's actually in the tabs. If the AI isn't
  available it falls back to a sensible heuristic name rather than failing.
- Sessions are listed **newest first** by default, and there's a sort picker if
  you'd rather see them another way.
- Each session shows badges for the browser, device and OS it came from.
- **Select** several at once (the same selection controls as
  [the library](/guides/library)) to delete them in bulk or export them to CSV.
  Every delete asks for confirmation first.

## Saved vs live

Saved sessions are durable snapshots you took on purpose. They stay until you
delete them and are included in your [data export](/guides/export-import).

They're distinct from [live sessions](/guides/live-sessions), which are an
opt-in, real-time mirror of your currently-open tabs that expires on its own and
is never exported.
