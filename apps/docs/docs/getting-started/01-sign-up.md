# Sign up

Bookmark AI runs at **[bookmark-ai.cloud](https://bookmark-ai.cloud)**.

1. Go to [bookmark-ai.cloud](https://bookmark-ai.cloud) and open the app.
2. Sign in. You can use email or **Google single sign-on**.
3. You land in your library — empty for now.

That's the account you'll use everywhere: the web app, the browser extension,
and the mobile and desktop apps all sign in with the same account and read the
same data.

## Set up your AI (optional but recommended)

The first time you open the app, an onboarding tour walks you through setup. The
very first step is connecting an AI provider, which powers **Ask AI** chat and
AI answers over your library.

You bring your own AI key. The quickest start is **OpenRouter**, which has free
models — see [AI providers](/guides/ai-providers) for the full walkthrough.

:::note
Search by meaning works without your own key — the embeddings that power it are
provided by the service for now. Your AI key is for chat and answers.
:::
