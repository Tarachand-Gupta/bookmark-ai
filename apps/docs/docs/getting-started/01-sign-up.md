# Sign up

Bookmark AI runs at **[bookmark-ai.cloud](https://bookmark-ai.cloud)**.

1. Go to [bookmark-ai.cloud](https://bookmark-ai.cloud) and open the app.
2. Sign in. There are four ways in:
   - **Email and password**.
   - **Email one-time code** — no password, we email you a code.
   - **Forgot your password?** — reset it with an emailed code.
   - **Google single sign-on**.
3. You land on your **dashboard** — Home. It's mostly empty for now, and it
   fills in as you save. See [Dashboard](/guides/dashboard).

That's the account you'll use everywhere: the web app, the browser extension,
and the mobile and desktop apps all sign in with the same account and read the
same data.

## AI is already on

The first time you open the app, an onboarding tour walks you through setup. Its
first step is AI — but there's nothing you have to do to make **Ask AI** work.

Every account includes **free AI credits**: 2,000 credits a week, which reset
every Monday at 00:00 UTC. No key, no billing, no setup. Chat is the only thing
that spends them, and you can see the meter any time in **Settings → AI**.

Bringing your own provider key is an optional upgrade rather than a requirement.
Do it if you want a specific model, or if you'd rather not be metered at all —
chat on your own key runs **unmetered**, and the free credits sit unused. The
quickest start is **OpenRouter**, which has free models. See
[AI providers](/guides/ai-providers) for the walkthrough.

:::note
Search by meaning needs no key of any kind, and never spends credits — the
embeddings that power it are provided by the service.
:::
