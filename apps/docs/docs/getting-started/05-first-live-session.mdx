# Share your first live session

A **live session** is a real-time mirror of the tabs you have open **right now**,
so you can jump to a page you left open on another device. Unlike a
[saved session](/getting-started/first-session), you don't snapshot anything —
the view updates live and expires on its own.

Live sessions are **off by default**. Nothing about your open tabs leaves your
browser until you turn sharing on.

## Turn it on

1. Open the Bookmark AI extension popup on the browser you want to share.
2. Flip **Share window as live session**.
3. **Name the device** so it's easy to recognize later (for example
   "Chrome on Mac" or "work laptop").

That browser now publishes its open tabs to the live server.

## See it appear live

Open the app on another device — or just another window of the same browser —
and go to **Live sessions** in the sidebar. Your device shows up with its open
windows, each window listing its tabs, and a green **LIVE** pip while it's
actively publishing. Open a new tab on the sharing browser and watch it appear
in the list within moments.

## Good to know

- **Off by default, opt-in per account.** Turning sharing on enables it; turning
  it off purges every device's live tabs immediately.
- **The green LIVE pip** marks a device that's actively publishing right now.
- **Private/incognito windows are never sent**, and credential-looking URLs are
  reduced to their origin before they leave your browser.
- **It expires.** Live tab data ages out automatically after **7 days** of a
  device going quiet — there's no long-term store of your open tabs.
- **Nothing is saved until you save it.** To keep a live tab, save it as a
  bookmark or snapshot the window as a saved session.
