# Bookmark AI

Bookmark AI saves a page from any browser and helps you find it again by what it
means — not just by the words you remember.

Save a bookmark from the browser extension and the app reads the page, pulls its
Open Graph data (title, description, image), sorts it into a category, tags it,
and builds a semantic embedding so you can search by meaning. Your library lives
on the web app, an iOS/Android app, and a desktop app — all reading the same
data.

## The 30-second tour

- **One-click saving.** Click the extension and the current page is saved. No
  folders to file it in — categorization and tags are automatic. You can also
  save from your phone's **share sheet**, and on Chrome and Firefox the pages you
  bookmark natively (the star button, Ctrl/Cmd+D) are mirrored into your library
  — along with anything you add to Chrome's **Reading List**.
- **A dashboard to land on.** The app opens on **Home**, where **Continue where
  you left off** surfaces the live device, saved session, or recent save you're
  most likely reaching for, next to recent saves, your reading list and a little
  activity. See [Dashboard](/guides/dashboard).
- **Search by meaning.** Search blends keyword matching with semantic similarity,
  so the right page surfaces even when you don't remember its exact title.
- **Ask AI.** Chat over your own library and get answers with citations back to
  your saved pages.
- **Free AI credits included.** Chat works with no setup and no API key:
  **1,000 credits a week**, resetting every Monday, where 1 credit is 1,000
  tokens of the conversation. Search-by-meaning embeddings and auto-categorization
  run on the service and never spend credits at all. Connecting your own provider
  key is an *optional* upgrade that makes chat unmetered — see
  [AI credits & providers](/guides/ai-providers).
- **Saved sessions.** Snapshot every tab in a window as one session, close them,
  and restore the whole window (or open tabs one at a time) later.
- **Live sessions.** Opt in and your open tabs mirror across your devices in real
  time — jump to a tab you left open on another machine.
- **Connect your AI agents.** Point Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any
  MCP client at your library so it can search, browse and save on your behalf.
  See [MCP](/guides/mcp).
- **Work in bulk.** Tick rows in the library to delete a batch or **Export CSV**
  of exactly what you selected. See [Library](/guides/library).
- **Your data is yours.** Export everything — bookmarks, saved sessions and your
  saved Ask AI conversations — to a single file and import it back. The whole
  project is open source and self-hostable.
